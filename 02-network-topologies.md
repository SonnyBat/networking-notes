# Star Topology

## Overview

A star topology connects devices individually to a central device, usually a switch.

Example:
PC
|
Switch
|
PC

## Advantages

- Highly scalable
- Easier troubleshooting
- Failure of one device does not affect others

## Disadvantages

- More expensive due to extra cabling and hardware
- Central switch failure can affect the entire network
